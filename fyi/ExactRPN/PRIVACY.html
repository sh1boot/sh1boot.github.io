Privacy Policy

Effective 15 November 2026

Exact RPN does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. It has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics, and no third-party tracking, and it does not request Internet access.

Your calculations are stored only on your device, so the app can restore them when you reopen it. They never leave the device, and uninstalling the app deletes them.

This policy covers the app itself. If you obtain Exact RPN through an app store, the store's operator may collect data under its own privacy policy; what a store collects is outside my control, and I receive no personal data about you from any store.
